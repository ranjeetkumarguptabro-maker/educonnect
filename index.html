“The more I study, the more insatiable do I feel my genius for it to be.”

About Her

Ada Lovelace was a brilliant mathematician who wrote the first algorithm intended for a machine. She is considered the world’s first computer programmer. Her imaginative thinking laid the foundation for modern computing.

Key Achievements

  • •Wrote the first computer algorithm (1843)
  • •Pioneered the idea of machines doing more than math
  • •Inspired future generations in science and technology

Why She Inspires

Ada showed that imagination and curiosity can open doors to a brighter future.
